# 🏆 Superteam Earn: MultiHopper Build & Route Submission Guide

This guide details the exact steps to finalize, deploy, and submit **MultiHopper Route Autopilot** to the **MultiHopper Build & Route Challenge** on Superteam Earn.

---

## 1. Submission Details
* **Listing URL:** [Superteam Earn MultiHopper Challenge](https://earn.superteam.fun)
* **Prize Pool:** $5,000 – $50,000 USDC + 0.25% perpetual revenue share
* **Deadline:** December 14, 2026
* **Qualification Metric:** ≥ $100,000 in Mainnet-Beta routed volume

---

## 2. Pre-Submission Checklist

- [x] **Core MultiHopper Protocol Integration:** Client handles fee estimation, route creation, and Token-2022 multi-hop execution.
- [x] **0.25% Revenue Share Rebate:** Automatic partner attribution on every transaction via `partnerId`.
- [x] **Batch Disperser Engine:** Staggered jitter routing to break timing correlation.
- [x] **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server:** Ready for autonomous agent integrations.
- [x] **Interactive CLI:** Built-in `estimate`, `disperse`, and `challenge` progress monitoring commands.
- [ ] **Mainnet Volume Target:** Route at least $100,000 through the live protocol before Dec 14, 2026.

---

## 3. How to Qualify & Win

### Volume Milestones
1. **$100,000 Volume:** Unlocks official qualification for the top-3 prize pool.
2. **$20,000,000 Ecosystem Milestone:** Expands the total prize pool up to the maximum $50,000 USDC.
3. **Guaranteed Revenue:** For every $100,000 in routed volume, you automatically earn **$250 USDC** in partner rebates straight to your wallet. For $1,000,000 routed, that's **$2,500 USDC**.

### Winning Strategy
- Integrate `multihopper-route-autopilot` into active DAO treasury runs or community bounty payouts.
- Connect the MCP Server to autonomous AI agents doing micro-transactions.
- Publish a short video walkthrough demonstrating the CLI dispersing payments and showing the intermediate Token-2022 hops on Solana Explorer.
